# Make the best-looking food picture — entry

A steaming bowl of ramen: chopsticks lifting glossy noodles, a jammy egg
and chashu, dark background. One look and you want to eat it.

## The image

`entry.png` — steaming bowl of ramen, chopsticks lifting glossy noodles,
jammy egg and chashu, dark background (JPEG bytes, 1024×1024, 234.742
bytes, under the 1 MiB cap; the name stays `entry.png`, the format is
told apart by its first bytes).

Why this entry: appetite first — one look and you want to eat it — then
fresh, real food with texture you can almost taste (steam, gloss), styling
and light that lead the eye to the dish, clean craft with no artefacts or
garbled text, and a single main dish that still reads at 300 pixels across.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the chosen image (see above).
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically
  (no network, no randomness, no clock), so both runs write identical bytes.
